   `hardware/veroroute/dk-perfboard-shield-layout.net` (generated by
   `make-layout-netlist.py`, re-run after schematic changes). Start with
   `nix-shell -p veroroute --run veroroute`, **Delete All Aliases**, then File →
   Import Netlist → OrcadPCB2. Mapping: `hardware/docs/perfboard-veroroute-aliases.md`.
   - Connectors are `SIPx` (1-row block, one hole per pin). `BLOCK_100MILx`
     (3-hole-tall pills) and `PADSx` (loose pads) are rejected.

   footprints), J1 is one DK symbol and net names contain parentheses. Its

3. **Changes made only in the layout netlist** (the schematic symbols are unchanged, as are the nets):
   - J3 LIS3DH becomes two `SIP6` rows **6 holes apart**: J3A = VIN 3Vo GND
     SCL SDA INT, J3B = I2 A3 A2 A1 SDO CS. The author read these from the
     breakout silkscreen.
   - **J2 e-paper HAT is now on a 2×20 RPi male header** (decision
     2026-11-03). J2A carries the odd RPi pins and J2B the even pins, 1 hole
     apart. Wiring: 3V3→pin 2, GND→6, RST 11, BUSY 18, DIN 19, DC 22, CLK 23,
     CS 24. **RPi pins 2/4 are the HAT power input; they get 3V3, never 5V.**
     Source: `hardware/docs/waveshare-hat-40pin.md`.

   pick-and-place rotations), DCF, Waveshare and 2N3904 EBC. See the
   "Checked against sources" table in the alias doc.
